Vehicle Radio Music Pack Kit

Make a pack in three steps:

1. Put your songs in the Input folder.
2. Open Music Pack Builder.exe and enter a pack name.
3. Click Build Pack. Your finished ZIP will be in Output.

MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC and M4A are supported. Python and FFmpeg are included;
you do not need to install either one.

Extract the entire kit before running it. Keep Tools beside the EXE.
Do not run the EXE from inside the ZIP or move it on its own.

ADD A COVER

Put cover.png, cover.jpg or cover.jpeg beside your songs.
A square 512 x 512 or 1024 x 1024 image works well. The builder crops other
shapes to a square and converts the image to a 512 x 512 game texture.
The same cover is also used as the mod manager preview. Covers are optional.

USE AND SHARE YOUR PACK

Import the ZIP into your mod manager and enable it alongside Bingus Shared
Loader and Vehicle Radio Framework. Each pack takes one slot in the wheel.
Selecting a pack plays a random song from it.

Share only the ZIP from Output, not this kit. Generated packs have no description.

UPDATE AN EXISTING PACK

Change the songs or cover, keep the same pack name, and build again.
Keep Tools/pack_ids.json when moving or updating the kit so the pack keeps
its identity. A different name creates a separate pack.

FOLDERS

Input   - Your songs and optional cover.
Output  - Finished music pack ZIPs.
Tools   - Files needed by the builder. Keep this folder intact.

If a build fails, check Tools/last_build.log.
